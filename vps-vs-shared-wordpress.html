VPS vs Shared Hosting for WordPress (2026)

The "cheap hosting" trap: what a $3 shared plan really costs once you grow.

Bottom line: if you're on a budget shared plan and your site is slow, a cheap 2 GB VPS will cost about the same per month as the shared host's "Pro" tier — and give you far more. The catch is you trade convenience (no cPanel, you manage updates) for control. For a WordPress site that gets any real traffic, the math usually favors a VPS.

Pick shared hosting if: you want zero server admin, one-click installs, and your traffic is under ~10k hits/mo.
Pick a VPS if: you want predictable performance, install WP-CLI and caching freely, and your traffic is growing.
