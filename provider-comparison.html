Vultr — the balanced pick

Vultr gives you a very large region network, predictable pricing, and a clean API. It's a strong default for most people and pairs well with the Docker stack most self-hosters run. Best for: a dependable all-rounder you can grow with.

DigitalOcean — the docs & ecosystem pick

DigitalOcean's tutorials, marketplace, and community are the best in the budget tier. If you're new to VPS admin and want the path of least resistance to self-hosting, DO is the friendliest on-ramp. Best for: beginners and anyone who lives off documentation.

RackNerd — the price pick (with caveats)

RackNerd undercuts everyone on raw price — the $1.5/mo entry is the cheapest real VPS you'll find. The honest tradeoff: budget hosts are more likely to oversell nodes, support is a ticket queue, and you get what you pay for. Best for: throwaway tests, a cheap reverse proxy, or a low-stakes side project you won't cry over. Don't run anything you depend on there.

Which one should you buy?

First VPS / self-hosting n8n or automation → Vultr or DigitalOcean. Pick Vultr if regions matter to you, DO if tutorials matter more.
Rock-bottom price → RackNerd. Just read the fine print on overselling.
You're running a 2+ GB workload → step up to a 2 GB plan on Vultr or DO rather than stacking $1.50 boxes; see our RAM sizing guide.
