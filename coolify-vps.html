Coolify on a Cheap VPS (2026)

Coolify is the open-source self-hosted PaaS. Here's how much VPS you actually need to run it and whether it's worth it vs a PaaS.

Bottom line: Coolify is light enough that a 2 GB VPS runs it well for a handful of apps. It's a genuinely great fit if you want Heroku-like deploys (Git push → live) without paying per-app pricing. You provide the VPS, Coolify handles the rest.

Spec guidance: Coolify itself is a small control plane. The RAM you really need depends on how many apps you deploy and what they are. 2 GB for a personal setup; go to 4 GB if you're running several services.

Why people run Coolify on a VPS

  • Git-based deploys: connect a repo, push, and it builds and deploys — no Docker files hand-rolled per app.
  • Database, reverse proxy, SSL handled — Caddy does TLS automatically.
  • You keep the box: all data on your VPS, no per-app or per-dyno pricing.

The install

Coolify installs from a single command on a fresh Ubuntu VPS with Docker:

curl -sSL https://coolify.io/install | bash

That sets up Docker, pulls Coolify, and starts the panel. You then point your DNS at the VPS, add a domain in the panel, and Coolify provisions HTTPS.
